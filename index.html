Professional Skills for Research Assistants

Slide decks and Try it tools for the SemiAcademy micro-credential Professional Skills for Research Assistants. They are shown inside the course pages in Canvas, and each one also opens on its own.

Slide decks

  • Module 1: Email habits and response times
  • Module 2: Writing to a project lead or teammate
  • Module 3: Meetings, attendance and reporting
  • Module 4: Research professionalism
  • Module 5: Time, tasks and project management
  • Module 6: Presenting your research
  • Module 7: Teamwork, feedback and mentorship
  • Module 8: Career readiness

Use the arrow keys or the buttons to move between slides. Printing a deck gives one slide per page.

Try it tools

  • Module 1: Reply, reply all or new thread?
  • Module 2: Pick the stronger message
  • Module 3: Weekly report builder
  • Module 5: Plan a week

The tools are for practice. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere, and they are not graded.
