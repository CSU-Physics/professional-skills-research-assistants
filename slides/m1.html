Email habits and response times

Know when to check email, how fast to answer, and where a reply belongs.

Professional Skills for Research Assistants · SemiAcademy

Module 1

What you will be able to do

  • Identify when to check email, based on your scheduled research days, and how fast to acknowledge and answer.
  • Decide when to reply in the existing thread and when a new thread is justified.
  • Choose between reply and reply all, and use cc, bcc and subject lines correctly.
  • Choose email, chat or phone for a message, and act correctly when something is urgent.

Decision 1 of 4

Check email on your scheduled days, and answer on time

  • Check at the start and end of every scheduled research day, and before every meeting.
  • Acknowledge within one working day. One line is enough.
  • Answer fully within two working days, or within the timeframe your supervisor agreed, or say when you will.
  • Asked for work by a date? Acknowledge now, then deliver by the date. The acknowledgement is not the delivery.
  • A working day is one of your scheduled research days.

Decision 2 of 4

Reply in the existing thread

  • Use Reply and keep the subject line, for example “Re: Shared notebook for run 14”.
  • A new thread is justified when the topic is materially different, the information is sensitive, or the audience is different.

Decision 3 of 4

Reply, reply all, cc, bcc and subject lines

  • Reply goes to the sender only. Use reply all only when everyone needs your answer.
  • Cc people who need to know, never to add pressure.
  • Bcc is only for large groups whose addresses should stay private.
  • A subject line says what the message is about: not “Help” or “Hi”.

Decision 4 of 4

Choose the channel, and know what to do when it is urgent

  • Email: requests, updates, anything to find later.
  • Chat: quick questions and short messages such as “I will be 10 minutes late”.
  • A call or conversation: urgent, sensitive, or not resolving. Then email a short summary of what was agreed.
  • Urgent: use the channel your supervisor gave you. Safety emergency: follow the emergency and safety procedure.

Worked scenario

Maya Brown's situation

Maya Brown works Wednesdays and Thursdays. On Wednesday at 9:05am she finds Dr. Lee’s Monday email, in the thread “Re: Shared notebook for run 14”, asking for her section by Thursday at 5pm. Sam has also asked the whole group who has the baseline file. Maya has it.

Before the next slide, decide what you would do, and why.

Worked scenario

Weaker and stronger

Weaker

A new email with the subject “hi” and the body “ok”. Then a reply all to the whole group with the baseline file.

Stronger

A reply inside the thread, subject unchanged: “Hi Dr. Lee, thanks, I have the notebook. I will add my section by Thursday at 5pm and send it back in this thread.” A reply to Sam only, with the file location.

Worked scenario

Why the stronger version works

Monday is not one of Maya’s scheduled days, so Wednesday is her first working day since the message. Answering on Wednesday morning is within one working day. The reply is in the thread, says what she will do and by when, and goes only to the people who need it.

Module 1

Remember

  • Acknowledge in one working day, answer in two.
  • Stay in the thread unless the topic, the sensitivity or the audience changes.
  • Reply all only when everyone needs it.
  • Urgent means the urgent channel, not email alone.

Try it this week

Put it into practice

Check your email at the start and end of each scheduled research day this week, and acknowledge anything addressed to you, inside its own thread, within one working day, and give the full response within the agreed timeframe.
