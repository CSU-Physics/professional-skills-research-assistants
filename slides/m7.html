Module 7 of 8

Teamwork, feedback and mentorship

Ask for help after a fair try, take and give feedback, and handle a disagreement or a missed deadline.

Professional Skills for Research Assistants · SemiAcademy

Module 7

What you will be able to do

  • Choose when and how to ask for help after a fair try.
  • Choose a constructive response to feedback, and a useful way to give it.
  • Choose a constructive response to a disagreement or a teammate’s missed deadline.
  • Identify good ways to help a newer student within the project’s rules.

Decision 1 of 4

Ask for help after a fair try

  • Try first: obvious causes, rerun, instructions, project notes. Write down what you tried.
  • Then ask a specific question and say what you tried.
  • Do not wait when equipment, data or safety is involved. Ask at once.

Decision 2 of 4

Take feedback well, and give it well

  • Receiving: listen fully, clarify what is meant, discuss relevant evidence respectfully, agree on next steps, and say what you will change and by when.
  • Giving: specific, about the work, not the person. Name one or two things and suggest how.
  • Discuss the work openly where the group works on it. Raise anything personal privately. Seek independent guidance for a safety, integrity or treatment concern.

Decision 3 of 4

Handle a disagreement or a missed deadline

  • Say your view and your reason, listen to theirs, look at the data. Still stuck? Ask your supervisor to decide, then follow the decision unless it raises a safety, integrity or treatment concern.
  • Disagree about the idea, not the person. Not in a group chat.
  • Teammate missed a deadline you depend on: message them privately first. If it will affect the work, tell your supervisor the facts.

Decision 4 of 4

Help a newer student within the project’s rules

  • Show once, point to the instructions, let them try. Do not do their work.
  • Do not share logins or data beyond what is approved.
  • Nobody uses equipment before required training is complete.

Worked scenario

Maya Brown's situation

Sam is due to add his section to the shared notebook by Thursday at 5pm. It is Thursday at 1pm, his section is empty, and Maya Brown needs it before Friday’s meeting. He is not late yet.

Before the next slide, decide what you would do, and why.

Worked scenario

Weaker and stronger

Weaker

Maya posts in the group chat: “Sam never does his part.”

Stronger

Maya messages Sam privately: “Hi Sam, are you on track to add your section by 5pm today? I need it to finish mine before Friday’s meeting. If you are stuck, I can look at it with you at 3pm. If it looks like it will slip past today, please tell me by 4pm so I can let Dr. Lee know and we can plan.”

Worked scenario

Why the stronger version works

Maya checks before the deadline has passed, offers help, and says what she will do and when. If Sam does not answer, or says he will miss Thursday, she can tell Dr. Lee the facts and what she needs, without blame, while there is still time to plan.

Module 7

Remember

  • Fair try, then a specific question.
  • Specific feedback about the work.
  • Privately first, supervisor if it affects the work.
  • Help newer students inside the rules.
