Module 8

What you will be able to do

  • Evaluate a resume line that describes research work.
  • Choose a good way to ask for a reference or a recommendation letter.
  • Identify sensible ways to find and track internships and programs.
  • Evaluate a short plan for the next two semesters.

Decision 1 of 4

Describe your research work in plain, specific terms

  • Start with a verb. Say what you worked on, the method, and the outcome with a number.
  • Describe your own role accurately. No claimed results, leadership or authorship you do not have.
  • No unpublished data, partner names or confidential details. Ask your supervisor first.

Decision 2 of 4

Ask for a reference or a recommendation letter

  • Ask at least two weeks ahead, and ask whether the person can write a strong letter.
  • First message: purpose, deadline, how it is submitted, your resume, a few lines about the work you did with them.
  • One reminder a week before. Thank them, and tell them the result.

Decision 3 of 4

Find internships and programs

  • Ask your supervisor and other faculty. Check career services and department announcements. Look at summer research programs for undergraduates and industry internships.
  • Keep a list with deadlines and requirements. Start from the earliest.
  • Apply early and tailor each application.

Decision 4 of 4

Make a two-semester plan

  • Three to five goals, each with a month or date.
  • Share it with a mentor and ask what they would change.
  • Review it monthly.

Worked scenario

Maya Brown's situation

Maya Brown plans to apply to a summer research program. The application is due Monday, February 15, 2027, and she has exams the week before. She wants Dr. Lee to write a letter.

Before the next slide, decide what you would do, and why.

Worked scenario

Weaker and stronger

Weaker

Subject “letter”. “can u write me a rec letter its due monday” Sent the Wednesday before the deadline.

Stronger

Sent Monday, January 25. Subject “Request for a recommendation letter for the summer research program (due February 15)”. “Dear Dr. Lee, I am applying to a summer research program for undergraduates, with the application due Monday, February 15. Would you be able to write a strong letter of recommendation for me? I have worked with you since August on the calibration project. I have attached my resume, a short summary of what I did, and the program description. If the submission link is not ready yet, I will send it as soon as I have it. Thank you, Maya Brown”

Worked scenario

Why the stronger version works

Maya asks three weeks ahead, asks whether Dr. Lee can write a strong letter, and attaches what she needs in the first message. She asks well before her exam week.

Module 8

Remember

  • Verb, task, number.
  • Two weeks ahead, with the details.
  • A list of deadlines.
  • Goals with dates, reviewed monthly.
